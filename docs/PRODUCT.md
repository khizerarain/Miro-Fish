## What it is
Miro Fish is an AI-powered virtual customer simulation platform. Founders, marketers, and product teams submit a product idea and test it on a population of AI-generated customer personas before spending money on real research or a launch.

## Inputs
- Product description (free text)
- Website URL (fetched and summarized server-side)
- Pricing plan (structured tiers)
- Ad copy or campaign (text, optional image)

## Core workflow
1. **Submit idea** — any combination of the inputs above.
2. **AI analysis** — the app extracts industry, category, target market, value proposition, features, pricing, competitors, and key assumptions. The user corrects anything wrong.
3. **Define audience** — AI-suggested segments with weights, custom segments, demographic filters, audience size (100–5,000).
4. **Generate personas** — diverse, seeded, reproducible synthetic customers with demographics, psychographics, needs, pain points, alternatives, and buying behavior.
5. **Run simulation** — each persona reacts: first impression, interest score, purchase intent, willingness to pay, price reaction, objections, wanted/missing features, what would convince them, and whether they'd recommend it.
6. **Explore results** — an interactive research dashboard: segment breakdowns, sentiment, clustered objections and feature requests, pricing analysis, messaging insights, prioritized recommendations, and a "How to validate this" panel. Export to PDF, CSV, or a share link.
7. **Talk to customers** — chat with any simulated persona, including a "convince me" mode.
8. **Compare** — A/B/C variants run against the same personas and seed.
9. **Improve and rerun** — apply recommendations, rerun, and track how results move over time. Import real survey data to reality-check the simulation.

## Honesty
All outputs are simulated hypotheses, not verified market research. The product labels results as simulated everywhere, explains this on first run, and every report includes concrete steps to validate findings with real customers.

## Audience
Early-stage founders, indie hackers, product marketers, and agencies who want fast directional signal on positioning, pricing, and messaging.
